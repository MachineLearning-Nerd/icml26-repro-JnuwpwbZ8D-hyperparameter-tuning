# C1 limitations and deviations

This is a proof-level reproduction, not an empirical pseudo-dimension estimate.
It does not re-formalize the several-hundred-page Basu-Pollack-Roy
quantifier-elimination algorithm or the complete Bartlett-Indyk-Wagner GJ
theorem from axioms. Instead, it explicitly treats their published theorem
statements—the same dependencies cited by the paper—as premises and
mechanically verifies the paper's claimed corollary.

The verdict is therefore a verification of Theorem 4.1 conditional on those
standard cited lemmas. It does not validate the inaccurate paraphrase in the
historical judge record.
