# Limitations and control

The result falsifies the literal displayed formulas only. It does not refute
the intended standard form with positive logarithmic guards. Replacing each
boundary logarithm by `log_2(1+z)` makes the witness's right-hand side order
`p`, compatible with its exact lower bound.

The finite enumeration through `p=8` is diagnostic. The proof is the
coordinate-wise identity
`I[(2y_i-1)/p>=0]=y_i`, which holds for every `p>=1`.
