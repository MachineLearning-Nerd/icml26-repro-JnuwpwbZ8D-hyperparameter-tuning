# Historical rejected baseline — archive

These pages are preserved byte-for-byte as historical evidence from the
original `6/12` release. They contain the formula substitutions, finite-grid
identities, self-consistency check, and small KKT examples that the evaluator
correctly classified as TOY.

They are not the current verification run and must not be used as the current
verifier. The superseding verifier is the six-claim empirical-signature stage
embedded directly on the CURRENT C1–C6 pages and executed by the gate embedded
on CURRENT — Release evidence and visibility.

The unchanged historical pages remain available as the children of this
archive node.
