## Exact theorem routes

- **Theorem 4.1:** the implementation substitutes the paper's polynomial-FOL
  atom count, degree, and quantifier-block dimensions into Theorem A.3. A
  halfspace class with known pseudo-dimension calibrates the pattern engine.
- **Theorem 5.1:** `K=1`, `d₁=d`, and
  `M=M_f+T_f+2d` reproduce the displayed training-loss bound.
- **Theorem 6.1:** two `d`-dimensional quantifier blocks reproduce the `d²`
  dependence, using a genuinely bi-level family with different training and
  validation objectives.
- **Theorem 7.2:** direct polynomial thresholding of a piecewise-rational
  optimizer path removes the quantifier-elimination dimension product.
- **Theorem 8.1:** Appendix G.1 contributes one block of dimension `d+2p`,
  `2(1+2p)` atoms, and degree two. The independent checker rejects the prior
  erroneous extra factor of `d`.
- **Theorem 8.2:** Proposition G.1 gives a full-rank convex box mp-QP path.
  The PSD Hessian and KKT residuals are checked numerically before counting
  regions.

reported as a domain ambiguity rather than silently ignored.
